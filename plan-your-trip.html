Travel Tips

  • Pack light, pack smart. Stick to a carry-on when you can, roll clothes instead of folding, and leave room for souvenirs on the way back.
  • Check entry requirements early. Visa rules, passport validity, and vaccination requirements can take weeks to sort out — check at least a month ahead.
  • Get travel insurance. Cover for medical emergencies, cancellations, and lost luggage is inexpensive compared to what it protects you from.
  • Keep digital and physical copies. Photograph your passport, ID, and bookings, and email them to yourself in case anything is lost.

Transportation

  • Flights. Book 6–8 weeks ahead for the best fares, and use flexible date search to find cheaper mid-week departures.
  • Airport transfers. Pre-book a transfer or confirm official taxi ranks in advance to avoid overpaying or unsafe pickups.
  • Getting around locally. Research whether your destination is best explored by public transit, rental car, or ride-hailing apps before you land.
  • Multi-city trips. For itineraries spanning several cities, compare regional rail passes against short domestic flights — trains often save time and money.

Best Time to Visit

  • Beach & island destinations. Aim for dry season months, and check rainfall and hurricane/monsoon patterns for your specific region before booking.
  • City breaks. Spring and early autumn usually offer mild weather and smaller crowds compared to peak summer.
  • Adventure & safari trips. Wildlife visibility and trail conditions often depend on the dry season — usually the best time to go.
  • Avoiding crowds. Traveling just before or after a destination's peak season ("shoulder season") often means better prices and fewer queues.
